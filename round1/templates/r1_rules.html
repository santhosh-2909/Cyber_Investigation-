KNOW THE GAME

Round 1 Rules

Setup
  • Each team registers with a unique Team ID.
  • Press Start to generate a random, server-assigned set of 6 challenges.
  • Your 6 are drawn from across the 12 cyber fundamentals categories.
  • Assignments are permanent: refreshing or logging back in resumes the same round.
Timing & Scoring
  • You have 30 minutes, enforced server-side. Warnings at 10, 5, and 1 minute.
  • Each challenge is worth 100 points (max 600).
  • Points are awarded only when the server validates a correct flag.
  • Resubmitting a solved challenge earns no extra points.
Flags & Answers
  • Submit the exact flag or answer the challenge requests.
  • Answers are matched case-insensitively and ignoring extra spaces.
  • Flags are stored only server-side and never revealed on the page.
  • Use the Hint button only if you are stuck.
Conduct
  • Do not share flags or answers with other teams.
  • The timer, scoring, and validation are all handled by the server — tampering is not possible.
  • Repeat submissions are rate-limited to prevent spam.
  • Have fun and solve like a pro.
